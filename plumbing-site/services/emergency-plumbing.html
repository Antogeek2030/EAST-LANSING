Fast 24/7 response for burst pipes, major leaks, and urgent plumbing failures.

While you wait for us, turn off the main water supply. The shutoff is usually in the basement or utility room near where the water line enters the house, often by the water meter. East Lansing MI Emergency Plumbing Services

1. Frozen and Burst Pipes

Michigan winters regularly drop below freezing. Exterior walls, crawl spaces, garages, and unheated basements are most at risk. If little or no water flows when you open a faucet, the pipe may be frozen. Close the main, then open the affected faucet. A frozen section can sometimes be warmed gradually with a hair dryer—never use an open flame, propane torch, or space heater aimed at the pipe. If you see cracks, bulging, or water flowing from the pipe, call us; a thawing split pipe will release water quickly. We thaw safely and repair or replace damaged sections. Related: leak detection and pipe repair.

2. Sewage Backups

If water is coming up through floor drains or several fixtures at once, stop using water so you do not add more volume to the system. Clay or cast-iron lines in older homes are vulnerable to root intrusion. We locate and clear the blockage and determine whether it is on your private line or the municipal main. If the same house has backed up before, a camera inspection helps show what is happening underground. See also our drain cleaning services.

3. Sump Pump Failure

During a storm, power outages often stop the sump pump. We diagnose and replace pumps that are leaking or broken and clear blocked discharge lines. For finished basements, a battery backup system keeps the pump running when power is out; we can install one.

4. Water Heater Emergencies

Call if the water heater is leaking heavily, making loud noises, or providing no hot water in the middle of winter. A leaking tank can fill a utility room quickly, and a failed heater leaves the home without hot water for bathing, cooking, and cleaning. Turn off the cold supply to the heater and the breaker (electric) or gas control (gas), then call us. We evaluate whether repair or replacement is the better option. More detail: water heater repair.

5. Overflowing Toilets

If one toilet is overflowing and will not stop, the issue is often local and can be resolved quickly. If multiple toilets overflow at the same time, the problem is usually in the main drain line. Stop using water and contact us. We clear the line, identify the cause, and tell you whether it is a one-time clog or a sign of something larger.

6. Flooded Basements

Flooded basements can result from a failed sump pump, a main-line problem, or a burst supply pipe. Do not wade in standing water if there is any chance of electrical hazard. We identify the cause and make the repairs so you can dry the space and limit mold risk.

7. Main Water Line Problems

No water at all, low pressure everywhere, or a wet spot in the yard with no recent rain can mean a leaking or broken main water line. These problems waste thousands of gallons and can damage foundations. We locate the break and repair it.

8. Gas Line Concerns

Plumbing and gas lines often share space around water heaters, furnaces, and stoves. If anyone smells gas, treat it as an emergency. Once the utility has confirmed the area is safe, we can inspect and repair gas connections so your home is secure.

Is there a 24-hour emergency plumber near me in East Lansing?

What should I do first if a pipe bursts?

Close the main water valve as soon as possible, open faucets to relieve pressure and drain the line, move items out of the water’s path, and shut off power to affected areas only if you can do so without standing in water. Then call us.

Why is sewage backing up in my basement?

Causes include a clog in your private sewer line, tree root intrusion in older clay or cast-iron lines, or a problem in the municipal main. If more than one fixture or a floor drain backs up at the same time, the blockage is typically in the main drain line. Stop using water and call a plumber.

Why is my water heater leaking or not making hot water?

No hot water can indicate a failed heating element, thermostat, or pilot/burner problem. Turn off the cold supply, cut power or gas, and hire a plumber. See water heater repair for more detail.
