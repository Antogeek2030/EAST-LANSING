Drain Cleaning Services in East Lansing, MI

Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning

Grease, food scraps, and coffee grounds harden inside kitchen lines over time. We clear the clog and flush the line so buildup is less likely to return in a few weeks.

Bathroom Sink, Tub, and Shower Drains

Hair, soap scum, and toothpaste residue build up in bathroom drains. We clear the blockage and clean the line. We do not simply push the clog farther down when you have standing water in the shower or a slow sink.

Toilet Clogs

If the toilet is clogged or will not flush after plunging, something may be stuck in the trap—wipes, excess paper, or another object. We determine whether the problem is in the toilet itself or farther down the line.

Main Sewer Line Cleaning

When several fixtures back up at once or sewage comes up through a floor drain, the main sewer line is often the cause. Root intrusion is common in older homes with clay or cast-iron pipe and nearby trees. We clear the line and help determine whether the blockage is on your private line or the municipal main. For related underground issues, see leak detection and pipe repair.

Drain Snaking

When a plunger is not enough, we use motorized cable machines to remove roots, hair, and debris. Snaking resolves many stubborn clogs, often the same day.

Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to remove grease, scale, sludge, and fine roots that a cable may leave behind. It is especially useful for persistent or heavy buildup. High pressure is not appropriate for every pipe—on older or fragile lines we inspect first to confirm the line can handle it.

Sewer Camera Inspection

A camera inspection lets us see inside the line: roots, cracks, sags where water collects, collapsed sections, or heavy buildup. You see exactly what is wrong and which repair makes sense.

Floor Drains and Basement Drains

Basement and laundry floor drains are often unused until they cause problems. A dry trap can let sewer gas in; a clogged drain can spill water across the floor during heavy rain or washing machine discharge. We clear the line and test that the drain works properly.

Emergency Drain Cleaning

If sewage is backing up or the main line is blocked and you have no usable drains, stop using water and call right away. Our emergency plumbing team responds quickly.
