Drain Cleaning in East Lansing

Professional clearing of clogged drains and sewer lines for lasting results.

Slow or completely blocked drains are one of the most common plumbing problems. Plumbing East Lansing offers thorough drain cleaning that removes the blockage and helps reduce the chance of quick return.

Drains We Clean

  • Kitchen sinks and garbage disposals
  • Bathroom sinks, showers, and tubs
  • Floor drains
  • Main sewer lines
  • Laundry and utility drains

Our Approach

We use the right tools for the job—snakes, hydro-jetting when needed, and inspection when appropriate—so the line is cleared properly rather than just temporarily opened. After the work we share simple maintenance tips tailored to your home.

When to Call a Professional

  • Water backs up into other fixtures
  • Multiple drains are slow at the same time
  • You notice foul odors from drains
  • DIY methods have not worked
