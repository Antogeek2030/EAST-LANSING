Fixture Installation

New faucets, toilets, showers, and sinks installed the right way.

Updating bathroom or kitchen fixtures improves both function and appearance. Plumbing East Lansing installs new fixtures carefully so they look great and operate without leaks or alignment issues.

Fixtures We Install

  • Kitchen and bathroom faucets
  • Toilets and bidets
  • Shower valves, heads, and systems
  • Sinks and vanity setups
  • Garbage disposals
  • Dishwasher and appliance connections

Our Installation Process

We protect your floors and surfaces, remove the old fixture cleanly, install the new one to manufacturer specifications and local code, check for proper operation and leaks, and leave the area tidy. If you are selecting new fixtures, we can offer practical advice on durable, water-efficient options that work well in East Lansing homes.

Why Professional Installation Matters

  • Correct sealing and connection prevent future leaks
  • Proper alignment and support extend fixture life
  • Warranty protection is often preserved
  • Cleaner, more professional finished look
