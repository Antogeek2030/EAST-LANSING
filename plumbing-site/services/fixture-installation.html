Fixture Installation Services

1. Faucet Installation

Faucets are among the most-used fixtures in the home and eventually wear out. We install:

  • Kitchen faucets with pull-down sprayers
  • Single-hole and widespread bathroom faucets
  • Bar and prep faucets
  • Laundry sink faucets

We also replace worn supply lines and shutoff valves at the same time—attaching a new faucet to a corroded line is a common cause of later leaks. Related: leak detection and pipe repair.

2. Toilet Installation and Replacement

Old toilets, cracked bowls, rocking bases, and constantly running water waste water and money. We remove the old toilet, inspect the flange and subfloor, install a new wax ring (or equivalent), set the toilet level, and test for leaks. Standard, comfort-height, and high-efficiency toilets all work reliably when installed correctly.

3. Sink Installation

We mount, seal, and connect drains for undermount, pedestal, and vanity sinks. Undermount sinks with stone countertops need careful sealing—an unsealed joint lets moisture into the cabinet below. We also install utility and laundry sinks in garages and basements.

4. Shower and Tub Valves, Trim, and Fittings

New showerheads, handheld showers, tub spouts, and valves can refresh a bathroom without a full remodel. We replace worn valves and cartridges, fix temperature-control problems, and install pressure-balancing valves to help prevent scalding when someone flushes a toilet elsewhere in the house.

5. Garbage Disposal Installation

Disposals must be securely mounted, sealed to the drain, and wired correctly. We install new units, replace failed ones, and make sure dishwasher drain lines are connected properly so wastewater does not back up into the sink. For related drain issues, see drain cleaning.

6. Appliance Hookups

Supply lines and drains are easy to get wrong and critical for safe appliance use. We connect dishwashers, refrigerator ice makers, and washing machines, and replace worn rubber hoses with braided stainless-steel lines that are far less likely to burst.

7. Hose Bibb / Outdoor Spigot Installation

Even an insulated outdoor faucet can crack or leak through the wall in a Michigan winter. Frost-free hose bibbs keep the shutoff point inside the heated part of the house. If you have had leaks around an exterior wall, our leak detection team can check for hidden damage.

8. Shutoff Valves and Supply Lines

The small valves under sinks and toilets are often ignored until they fail. We replace seized shutoffs and worn supply lines so each fixture can be isolated in an emergency without shutting off the whole house.

9. Specialty Fixtures and Upgrades

We install touchless faucets, accessible and ADA fittings, water-conserving fixtures, and bidets or bidet seats. If you are unsure which options fit your home and lifestyle, we will discuss the practical choices with you.
