Water Heater Repair & Installation

Reliable hot water again—repair or replace with confidence.

Whether your water heater is leaking, making noise, or no longer producing hot water, Plumbing East Lansing can diagnose the issue and recommend the most cost-effective solution.

Services Include

  • Repair of gas and electric tank water heaters
  • Tankless water heater service and repair
  • Full replacement and installation of new units
  • Expansion tanks, pressure relief valves, and related components
  • Energy-efficient upgrades

Repair or Replace?

We evaluate age, condition, and cost of repair versus a new unit. Many homeowners in East Lansing choose to upgrade to a more efficient model when the old tank is near the end of its life. We help you compare options clearly so you can decide with confidence.
