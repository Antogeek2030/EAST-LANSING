Leak Detection & Pipe Repair

Locate and fix leaks accurately to protect your home and lower water bills.

Hidden leaks can go unnoticed for months, causing higher bills, mold risk, and structural damage. Plumbing East Lansing uses proven methods to locate the source of leaks and complete reliable repairs.

Types of Leaks We Address

  • Supply line leaks under sinks or behind walls
  • Slab and underground leaks
  • Toilet and faucet leaks
  • Water heater and appliance connections
  • Irrigation and exterior leaks

How We Find Leaks

Depending on the situation we may use visual inspection, pressure testing, acoustic listening equipment, or other non-destructive methods. Once located, we explain the repair plan and complete the work with minimal disruption.

Benefits of Early Detection

  • Lower water and energy costs
  • Prevention of mold and water damage
  • Avoidance of larger, more expensive repairs later
