Leak Detection & Pipe Repair

Locate and fix leaks accurately to protect your home and lower water bills.

Call us at (858) 341-1138 for leak detection and pipe repair in East Lansing, MI. We offer the following services when you notice:

  • A pipe that has a leak or rupture and is running water
  • A sudden increase in your water bill that you cannot explain
  • The sound of running water when all fixtures are turned off
  • Wet or discolored drywall, ceiling stains, or peeling paint
  • Areas of warmth or wetness on the floor (possible slab leak)
  • Low water pressure throughout the house
  • Musty smell or mold in a basement, closet, or crawl space
  • A wet lawn when it has not rained

Our Leak Detection and Pipe Repair Services in East Lansing

1. Hidden Leak Detection

Damage from leaks in walls, ceilings, and floors can be devastating. We do not cut open drywall to guess where moisture is coming from. We use non-invasive methods such as acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging, and moisture meters to locate the source. Accurate location means smaller repairs, less debris, and lower cost.

2. Slab Leak Detection and Repair

When homes are built on concrete slabs, leaks can develop in piping under the house. Warning signs include a warm floor, water running under the slab, bill spikes, and unexplained wetness. Slab leaks can damage flooring, foundations, and surrounding soil. We identify the leak and discuss repair options.

3. Burst and Frozen Pipe Repair

Michigan winters get cold. Pipes in exterior walls, crawl spaces, garages, and unheated basements are vulnerable. If water only trickles from a faucet, a pipe may be frozen. Cracks, bulging, or active leaking after a freeze mean you need immediate help—thawing a split pipe can release a lot of water at once.

4. Pinhole Leaks and Corroded Pipes

Older homes often have galvanized steel or aging copper piping. Corrosion can create small pinhole leaks that spray or drip undetected. One pinhole often means more are on the way. We patch the damaged area and evaluate whether a larger section (or full repipe) is the smarter long-term solution.

5. Repiping

When leaks keep returning, pipes are heavily corroded, water is rusty, or pressure keeps dropping, fixing one leak at a time can cost more than starting fresh. We explain the scope, timeline, and cost of replacing worn lines with durable modern piping before any work begins.

6. Main Water Line Maintenance and Repair

Low pressure throughout the house, no water at all, or a wet spot in the yard with no recent rain can point to a leaking or broken main line. These leaks waste thousands of gallons and can harm your foundation. We locate the break and repair or replace the damaged section.

7. Sewer and Drain Line Leaks

Not every leak is clean water. Clay and cast-iron lines can crack or become invaded by roots, allowing sewage to escape into the ground or back up into the house. Symptoms include slow drainage, gurgling, unpleasant odors, and patches of lush, wet vegetation in the lawn. Camera inspection shows the condition underground so repairs target the real problem. For related drain work, see our drain cleaning services.

8. Leaks from Fixtures, Faucets, and Water Heaters

A leaky supply line, broken valve, or sink connection can ruin cabinets and floors. We repair or replace the failing part before a small drip becomes a large problem. If the leak is at the water heater, our team can help—see water heater repair for more detail.

9. Emergency Leak Repair

If a pipe is flooding your home, turn off the water and contact us right away. Our emergency plumbing service responds quickly so damage is limited.
