How to Prevent Clogged Drains

March 12, 2026 · Plumbing Tips

Clogged drains are one of the most frequent service calls we receive in East Lansing. Many of them can be avoided with a few consistent habits.

In the Kitchen

  • Never pour cooking grease or oil down the drain. Collect it in a container and dispose of it in the trash once cooled.
  • Use a sink strainer to catch food scraps and empty it into the trash regularly.
  • Run cold water while using the garbage disposal and for a few seconds afterward.
  • Avoid putting fibrous foods (celery, potato peels, coffee grounds in large amounts) down the disposal.

In the Bathroom

  • Install hair catchers in showers and tubs; clean them weekly.
  • Do not flush anything other than toilet paper and human waste.
  • Wipe excess toothpaste and hair from the sink before rinsing.

General Maintenance

Once a month, pour a kettle of hot (not boiling if you have PVC pipes) water down drains to help dissolve mild buildup. For deeper cleaning, a mixture of baking soda and vinegar followed by hot water can help, but it is not a substitute for professional cleaning when lines are significantly restricted.

If a drain is already slow or completely blocked, call a professional rather than repeatedly using harsh chemical cleaners, which can damage pipes and rarely solve the root cause.
